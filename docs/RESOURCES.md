## How to use a resource in 30 minutes
1. Read one small section for 10 minutes.
2. Reproduce one safe example for 15 minutes.
3. Write what you learned and one question for 5 minutes.

## Device-modification caution

Root, bootloader unlocking, recovery and custom-ROM installation can erase data, weaken security, or leave a device unable to boot if the wrong image is used. Confirm the exact codename, firmware base, Android version, partition layout and maintainer instructions. A guide for a related device may not be interchangeable.
